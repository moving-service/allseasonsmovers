How Can You Prepare for a Same-Day Move?

Start by separating items that will move from items that will not. Then remove personal documents, jewelry, cash, medication, and other items you prefer to transport yourself.

Next, keep pathways clear. Remove small objects from floors and stairs. If possible, secure pets in a separate area during loading.

You should also check whether your building requires elevator reservations or loading arrangements. Apartment buildings may have their own moving rules, so checking these details early can prevent unnecessary waiting.

Finally, keep your phone available on moving day. A quick response can help the crew confirm arrival details or access information.
